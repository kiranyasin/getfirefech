How it works

Three steps. No sign-up.

Paste

Copy the share link from the app or site and drop it in. Pasting starts the fetch on its own.

Pick

Video with sound, sound only, or video with no sound. Quality options depend on what the site offers.

Save

The file downloads to your device, named after the video's title. Nothing is kept on our server.
